You can use a foreign passport to book a train on China Railway’s English website. Have your passport open and use an email address you can check. The activation link confirms your email address. To see whether the railway has also verified your passport, sign in and check your personal information. [Register on the English 12306 website](https://www.12306.cn/en/register.html)

## Enter your name as it appears in your passport

The English form has one **Name** box. If your passport labels your family name “Surname” and your other names “Given names,” enter the surname first, then a space, then your given names in order. For example, a passport showing **Surname: MARTIN** and **Given names: ANA SOFIA** would give **MARTIN ANA SOFIA**. If your passport groups your names differently, open the illustrated passport guide beside the Name box and find the example that matches your document. [12306 registration form and illustrated guide](https://www.12306.cn/en/register.html) · [China Railway’s passport entry examples](https://mobile.12306.cn/otsmobile/h5/otsbussiness/passportInstruction/passport-entry-instructions.html)

Copy your nationality, passport number, expiry date, date of birth and gender from the passport you’ll travel with. Choose a username and password, then enter your email address. The first screen of the English website asks for email. If you use a registration option that asks you to verify a mobile number, follow its SMS instructions. [English registration form](https://www.12306.cn/en/register.html) · [Beijing Foreign Affairs Office registration guide](https://wb.beijing.gov.cn/home/index/wsjx/202401/t20240115_3535596.html)

## Activate your account and check your passport status

Open the activation email from 12306 and follow the link. If it hasn’t arrived, check your spam folder, then sign in and request another activation email. On the website, open **My 12306** and select **User information** to check your identity status. [12306 email activation instructions](https://www.12306.cn/en/register-success.html) · [Official guide to email activation and identity checks](https://wb.beijing.gov.cn/home/index/wsjx/202401/t20240115_3535596.html)

- **Verified:** Search by departure station, arrival station and travel date. Choose your train, select yourself as the passenger and check the details before paying. Bring the same original passport to the station for boarding. [China Railway booking](https://www.12306.cn/en/index.html) · [China Railway ticketing FAQ](https://www.12306.cn/en/faq.html?item=1)
- **Pending (待核验):** Check what the booking screen offers for your passenger record. China Railway’s help pages give different answers about buying a ticket while a foreign passport is pending. If checkout is blocked and you need to travel soon, take your passport to a staffed station ticket counter. [Online booking FAQ, question 9](https://kyfw.12306.cn/otn/gonggao/onlineBooking.html) · [Identity verification FAQ, question 19](https://kyfw.12306.cn/otn/gonggao/checkIdentity.html?no=1)
- **Failed (未通过):** Compare the saved details with your passport and correct any mismatch. If 12306 offers photo verification for your account, follow the upload prompt. It can ask for the passport photo page and a second photo of you holding it open to that page. [Official photo-verification walkthrough](https://wb.beijing.gov.cn/home/index/wsjx/202401/t20240115_3535596.html)

## If you need help at the station

Go to a staffed ticket counter (**售票窗口**) with the original passport you used to register. You can show the clerk this sentence: **我想用这本护照办理铁路 12306 身份核验。** It means “I’d like to verify my identity for 12306 using this passport.” If you also want to buy a ticket there, have your departure station, destination and travel date ready. [Official English guide to station verification and ticket purchase](https://english.beijing.gov.cn/specials/aguidetoworkingandlivinginchinaasbusinessexpatriates2024/dailylifeservices/202404/t20240424_3635102.html)
